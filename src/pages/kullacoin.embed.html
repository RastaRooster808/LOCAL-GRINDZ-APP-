Why this works

The model is 50 years proven. In 1975 the musician Harry Chapin and DJ Bill Ayres founded WhyHunger — turning music revenue directly into food programs. KullaCoin makes it personal: your purchase becomes a song, and the song funds the pantry.
Lineage: WhyHunger (1975) → music as a capital engine for food.

The line that keeps it safe: KULLA is earned, never sold for cash, never cashed out. That makes it a closed-loop rewards program — like airline miles or a punch-card — not a currency to speculate on. We keep it there on purpose.
